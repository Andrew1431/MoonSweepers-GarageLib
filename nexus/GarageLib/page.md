# GarageLib

- **Name:** GarageLib
- **Summary (≤ 350 chars):** Vehicle library for Moon Sweepers: lets other mods add new vehicles to the garages, saves them
  without touching your vanilla save, and gives ECO suction a speed bonus. Needed by Late Game Vehicles.
- **Category:** Utilities (or Modders Resources)
- **Version:** 0.2.0
- **File:** `dist/GarageLib-0.2.0.zip`, main file, "Unzip into the game folder".
- **Requirements:** BepInEx be.791 (off-site), MoonSweepers BepInEx Patcher (Nexus mod 9).
- **Mods requiring this file:** Late Game Vehicles (add once its page exists).
