# Shared Upgrades

- **Name:** Shared Upgrades
- **Summary (≤ 350 chars):** Vehicle upgrades are bought once for the whole team and apply to every vehicle, including
  ones you buy later. Turning it on raises everyone to the best level anyone has; removing it keeps those levels.
  Prices scale with your biggest vehicle.
- **Category:** Gameplay
- **Version:** 0.1.0
- **File:** `dist/SharedUpgrades-0.1.0.zip`, main file, "Unzip into the game folder".
- **Requirements:** BepInEx be.791 (off-site), MoonSweepers BepInEx Patcher (Nexus mod 9). GarageLib is not required.
