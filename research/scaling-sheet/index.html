Patterns in the sweeper line

Sweep rate (width × speed) barely moves after the Lunar sweeper, while the hopper keeps growing at the price's rate. Past the mid-game, money buys capacity, not speed. The prototypes follow the same rule: bigger cargo always costs speed and turning.

Upgrade prices

Every upgrade costs its base price × the vehicle's upgrade multiplier. Crew transport skips the sweeper-only upgrades.

Vehicle lines

Each line branches off a vanilla vehicle and multiplies its stats every tier. Pick a line to tune it; every chart below compares all lines against the vanilla sweepers. Changes stay in your browser until you save them for everyone.
