# Comet hoover (`andrew1431.comet-hoover`)

Fast line tier 3, after the Streak sweeper. 4.66 m sweep, 9.1 m/s, 32,900 kg hopper, $94,000.

## Pitch

A prototype racer about 8 m long and barely knee-high at the nose: a long, low white hull pinches in from a huge
jet-intake mouth down to a narrow body, then rises into a hopper hump and twin raked tail fins with a wing between them.
The gimmick is that the intake is the vacuum head. The full 4.66 m mouth, rimmed in cyan, swallows dust over two orange
rollers, so from the front it looks like a jet engine eating the regolith. Small front wheels tucked behind the scoop,
huge exposed rear wheels, a bubble canopy and fins make it read as a race car more than a work truck. That puts it one
step sleeker and faster-looking than the Streak, without yet being the tier-4 land-speed car. The hopper fairing behind the
cockpit is the bulkiest part of the car, which shows the 32.9 t capacity.

## Parts

- Lofted hull (8-sided sections) flaring from a 4.8 m intake duct to a 2.3 m body; black under-tray and skirts
- Jet-intake scoop: white top lip with headlights and orange stripe, white side cheeks, black splitter and back wall,
  cyan rim lights on top and sides
- Two orange rollers inside the mouth, each exactly 4.66 m (`width` stat): a 0.22 m ground roller and a 0.15 m pickup
  roller, with black cores and three thin guide vanes
- Cockpit: black tub, racing seat, steering wheel, dark-glass bubble canopy
- Hopper fairing hump behind the cockpit, with an orange lid stripe, cyan fill-gauge strips, black vents and a dump door
- Twin canted tail fins with orange tips and cyan lamps, and a black wing across them
- Tail: black diffuser, twin exhausts with a cyan glow, orange tail lamp, antenna with an orange tip lamp
- Wheels: 0.46 m fronts under black fenders with orange stripes, 0.64 m exposed rears, all with light hubs and black axle stubs
- Livery: orange double stripe on each flank, twin nose-deck stripes, a cyan running light on each side

## Numbers

- Export: 138 nodes, 6 materials, **18,628 triangles**
- Footprint about 4.9 m wide (the scoop) x 8.0 m long x 2.5 m tall (antenna); about 2.2 m to the wing

## Would improve

- The wheels from `kit.wheel` (beveled tread blocks) are roughly half the triangle budget. A cheaper tread would leave
  room for a smoother canopy and hull.
- The canopy is a plain scaled sphere. A teardrop with a frame and a visible driver would read better up close.
- The hull loft is faceted. A subdivided or more finely sectioned nose would make it look more like a racer.
- The scoop's side cheeks are flat plates. Shaped nacelle-style cheeks that blend into the duct would sell the jet-intake idea.
- The rollers are static cylinders. Bristle texture or segmentation, plus a spin hook in-game, would help.
