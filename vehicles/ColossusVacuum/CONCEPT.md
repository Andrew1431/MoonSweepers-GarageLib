# Colossus vacuum (`andrew1431.colossus-vacuum`)

Wide line, tier 2. 7.78 m sweep strip, 3.9 m/s, 18,750 kg hopper, $37,500.

## Pitch

A three-axle municipal vacuum truck: a tall forward cab, then a huge white drum tank, about 5 m long and 2.8 m across,
sitting on black saddles. That is roughly six times the Mega rover's hopper box. Out front, a full-width suction head
spans exactly 7.78 m in three hinged sections: a centre head plus two wings on hydraulic rams, all with orange roller
brushes, and a disc brush at each wing tip. The gimmick is the "elephant trunk": a fat ribbed black hose with white
and orange bands. It rises from the suction plenum, curls up past the right side of the cab and over the roof, and
plunges into the front of the tank. You can see the dust's whole path from across the map. It reads as a step up from
the Titan: wider brushes, one more axle, a much bigger hopper and a slow, heavy, top-tall stance (about 6 m tall at
the antenna, 3.3 times the height of the 1.8 m astronaut).

## Parts

- Chassis: black ladder frame, white side skirts with orange stripes, front and rear bumpers
- 6 chunky tyres (r 0.68 m) on three axles, light hubs, 12 tread lugs each, black arches
- Cab: white two-tier cab, big dark windscreen and side windows, black roof, orange beacons, cyan light bar, mirrors,
  headlights and grille, steel ladder; driver seat, dash and steering wheel inside
- Hopper: white drum tank with two orange bands and a decal stripe, front cap, rear dump door with ring, hinge, latches
  and twin rams; roof walkway, hatch and handrail; twin stacks; antenna with orange tip; cyan side lights
- Suction head: centre section (3.0 m) plus two hinged wings (2.39 m each), so the three rollers span 7.78 m; black
  hoods with orange stripes, steel hinge pins, wing-tip disc brushes, skids and lamps, wing rams, lift arms
- Trunk duct: Bezier-routed 18-segment hose with banded ribs, orange collars at the plenum and tank, steel strut to
  the cab roof

Triangles (export line): **16,116** (238 nodes, 7 materials).

## Would improve

- The wheel arches are flat black shelves. Shaped fenders that follow the tyres would read better.
- The suction head is very boxy in side view. A sloped front lip or a skirt of black flaps would soften it.
- The wing rams join at the bumper and look a bit tacked on. A proper hinge bracket on the head would fix that, and
  the wings could get a folded-up transport pose (separate objects pivoting on the hinge pins) for animation.
- The tank could use a slight taper or domed end caps to look less like a plain cylinder.
- The disc brushes at the wing tips are small and hard to see from most angles.
