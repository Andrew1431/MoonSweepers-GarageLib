# Lunar buggy

An open-frame runabout built off the Ride-on sweeper: a low white tub, a steel roll cage with a cyan light bar, four
balloon tyres on a wide stance, and orange fenders. The 1.27 m roller brush rides out front on two arms, and a tiny
orange-lidded hopper sits behind the seat with a whip antenna. It reads as the fun one: fast, twitchy, nearly useless
for hauling, great for zipping around the base.

antenna, four treaded wheels, fenders, front roller brush with hood and arms, headlights.
