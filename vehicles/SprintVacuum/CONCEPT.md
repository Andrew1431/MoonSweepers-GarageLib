# Sprint vacuum (`andrew1431.sprint-vacuum`), Fast line tier 1

## Pitch

The Mega vacuum rover after a weekend in a hot-rod garage: a white street sweeper cut down to 1.95 m tall, with a
wedge nose, a low raked glass cab and a fastback hopper that slopes off behind the driver. Its gimmick is the
"front wing": a full 4.0 m orange roller brush carried ahead of the nose on struts, with white endplates and orange
marker lamps, so it sweeps like a race car slicing air. Twin orange racing stripes run nose to tail, chrome vacuum
side-pipes feed the hopper like hot-rod exhausts, and fat exposed rear wheels (1.06 m vs 0.8 m fronts) give it a
raked, ready-to-go stance. It is still plainly a work truck (beacon, antenna, black bumpers, dump door), which is
why it reads as tier 1 of the Fast line rather than the land-speed car at the end of it.

## Parts

- `SprintVacuum_Root` empty, everything parented under it; nose at -Y, wheels on Z = 0
- Black chassis spine and side skirts
- White wedge nose with black intake, orange headlights and a cyan accent strip
- Black front wheel arches; black rear arches over exposed rear wheels
- Low cab: white tub, raked dark glasshouse, thin white roof, black cowl trim, mirrors, driver seat inside
- Fastback hopper: narrow lower bin between the rear wheels, wide sloping upper shell, black dump door, ducktail lip,
  rear bumper, tail lamps
- Orange livery: twin racing stripes (nose, deck, roof, fastback) and side stripes on cab and hopper
- Steel vacuum side-pipes with black clamps, running from the nose back into the hopper sides
- Roof beacon (orange lamp) and black antenna with lamp tip
- Wheels: 0.40 m radius fronts, 0.53 m x 0.44 m chunky rears, white hubs
- Brush bar: 4.0 m orange roller (= width stat) in a black hood, white endplates with stripes and lamps, black arms,
  struts and centre duct

Overall about 6.3 m long including the brush bar, 4.2 m across the endplates, 1.95 m to the roof (2.5 m to the
antenna tip); body 1.84 m wide.

## Numbers

Export: 145 nodes, 7 materials, **17,320 triangles** (`sprint_vacuum.bin`).

## Would improve

- Most of the triangle budget goes into the bevelled tread blocks on the wheels; dropping bevels on treads would
  free room for detail elsewhere.
- The glasshouse is one glass wedge; white A/B pillars and a door seam would make the cab read more like a car.
- The rear arches are flat plates; curved arches (and a small spoiler) would push the sporty read further.
- The 4 m bar is cantilevered on thin struts; a slightly beefier mount or a hinge detail would sell its weight.
- Hopper volume (~4.5 m3) is plausible for 5250 kg but could gain a visible vent or level window.
