# Mare harvester (`andrew1431.mare-harvester`)

Wide line tier 4: 16.36 m sweep strip, 3.0 m/s, 117,500 kg hopper, $235,000.

## Pitch

A combine harvester for the Moon. A 16.36 m header of orange rollers and disc brushes hangs from two trussed booms,
held up by suspension-bridge stays from a pair of masts, so from the front the machine reads like a sweeping bridge
crawling across the mare. Behind it a 12.6 m silo hopper with a catwalk ring towers over everything. It rides on four crawler tracks with the cab perched on a tall black
pedestal above the header, so the driver climbs a ladder to reach it. Every Wide-line trait (more brushes, longer booms,
bigger hopper, more running gear) is pushed to the point of absurdity: the crawling sweeping factory that tops the line.

## Parts

- Chassis: white deck 4 x 10 m, black skirt, double orange side stripes, rear bumper.
- Running gear: four black crawler tracks (steel idlers, road wheels, top-run grousers), white fenders with orange tabs.
- Header (16.36 m, nose at -Y): white header beam with orange stripe; four gangs of orange roller brushes in black hoods
  with end plates, spanning exactly the sweep width; 12 orange disc brushes on black arms in front, edge to edge across
  the strip; black feeder housing to the chassis.
- Booms: two trussed booms (white top chord, steel verticals and diagonals), two white masts with orange beacons and
  steel stays to mid-span and tip, braces back to the cab pedestal; wingtip castor wheels, black tip posts with orange
  beacons, cyan marker lamps.
- Cab: white cab on a 2.5 m black pedestal, large tinted windscreen and side glass, orange stripes, roof with black
  light bar and four orange work lamps, two orange beacons, antenna; seat, seat back and console inside; steel ladder
  and black landing with orange rail on the left.
- Hopper: white base box with orange stripes and cyan fill gauges; 5.4 m white silo with black collar, two orange bands,
  tinted window strip, cyan lamps, conical cap, hatch, big orange beacon, antenna; catwalk ring with orange railing;
  side ladder.
- Engine pod: white pod with black grille and steel slats, twin steel exhaust stacks, orange tail lamps, cyan accents,
  orange deck handrails.

## Numbers

- Export: 334 nodes, 7 materials, **15,372 triangles** (`mare_harvester.bin`).
- Bounds roughly 16.4 m wide (the header), 15.3 m long, 13.6 m tall to the antenna tip.

## Would improve

- Tracks are boxes with end drums; a proper track belt with grousers all round (or a few bogie wheels) would read better
  close up.
- The roller gangs are mostly hidden behind the disc row from the front three-quarter view; staggering the discs or
  lifting the hoods would show more orange.
- The header could be split into hinged wing nodes so the booms can fold up for a "road mode".
- A closer preview shot next to a track would sell the size better.
