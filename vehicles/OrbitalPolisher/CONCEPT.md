# Orbital polisher (`andrew1431.orbital-polisher`)

Fast line, tier 4. Stats: 5.03 m sweep strip, 11.1 m/s, 82,250 kg hopper, $235,000.

## Pitch

A land-speed-record needle, 8.8 m long and only 1.5 m across the body, that drags a 5.03 m vacuum blade in front of
it like a hammerhead wing. Behind the bubble canopy, a dorsal hopper spine with an orange lid sweeps back into a 2.85 m
stabiliser. Huge exposed rear wheels sit on faired outrigger arms, and three steel rocket nozzles glow orange from
inside. The gimmick is the blade: a full-width orange roller in a black hood, under a white wing with raked black
endplates. Cyan underglow runs along the flanks. The rest of the Fast line gets lower and sleeker; this tier stops
looking like a sweeper and starts looking like a record attempt, but it keeps the white, black and orange livery.

## Parts

- Lofted needle fuselage (white) with a black lower hull shell, twin orange nose racing stripes, and an orange flank
  stripe on each side
- Cyan underglow strips on both flanks, plus a cyan glow bar under the blade and on each endplate
- Bubble canopy (dark glass) with a black seat, seat back and yoke inside (the driver position)
- Dorsal hopper spine (white) with an orange lid band and black lid seams
- Vertical stabiliser (white) with an orange tip, a black band and an orange tail lamp
- Three rocket nozzles (steel bell, black rim and throat, orange lamp core) on a black tail plate
- Front vacuum blade: 5.03 m orange roller brush (`kit.roller_brush`, length = width stat) in a black hood, black
  suction mouth, white wing with an orange stripe, black raked endplates with orange marker lamps, twin black pylons,
  and a black intake duct into the nose
- Front wheels (r 0.40 m) in white spats with orange stripes, on black wishbones
- Rear wheels (r 0.85 m, 0.52 m wide) on white faired arms with orange stripes and black lower links
- Black antenna with an orange tip lamp
- Root empty `OrbitalPolisher_Root`

## Numbers

Export: 126 nodes, 7 materials, **17,612 triangles**. Envelope about 5.23 m wide (blade) x 8.8 m long x 2.85 m tall
(fin); canopy top about 1.65 m.

## Would improve

- An 82 t hopper can't really fit in a needle. The spine reads as "hopper", but not as one that size. Options: a
  detachable pod trailer, or a "compressed dust canister" decal story.
- Rear outrigger arms are thin (one fairing and one link). A proper upper and lower wishbone pair would look sturdier.
- The roller is hard to see in the hero shot, under the wing. A row of small disc brushes peeking out at the blade
  tips would add some vanilla flavour.
- No decals (race number, Fast line chevrons). The fin and the spats are natural places for them.
- The nozzle glow only shows from behind. The stock previews don't include a rear view, so I checked it with a one-off
  render.
